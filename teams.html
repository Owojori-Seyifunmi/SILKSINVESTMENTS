BUILDING WEALTH SINCE 2003

Silks Investments Ltd was founded with a clear purpose: to help individuals create sustainable wealth through safe, strategic property investments. Our journey began in 2003 with our first investment on The Palm, Jumeirah, and has since grown into over 20 years of global property experience across markets including Dubai, Malaysia, Bulgaria, Turkey, New Zealand, Spain, and the UK.

With this extensive knowledge, we support both new and experienced investors in building profitable portfolios rooted in clarity and confidence. We specialise in sourcing below-market-value, high-yield investment opportunities, and we believe that knowing your investment strategy and exit before you buy is the foundation of low-risk, successful investing—whether the goal is short-term value creation, steady cash flow, or long-term generational wealth.

Beyond sourcing deals, education sits at the heart of what we do. Through our mentoring and coaching programmes, we help investors overcome limitations, develop the right mindset, and take informed action—regardless of starting capital or credit history. At Silks Investments Ltd, we don't just provide opportunities; we guide you every step of the way as you unlock your wealth through property.
